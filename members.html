Principal Investigator

Bong-Goon Seo 서봉군

Assistant Professor, Department of Management Information Systems, Hannam University
Ph.D. in Management Engineering (Management Information Systems), Kookmin University

Prof. Seo studies digital business from the perspective of consumer and user behavior — including psychological ownership in online services, UX/CX, data-driven insight, and science, technology, and digital policy. Before joining Hannam University, he was a Senior Researcher at KISTI and served as an expert advisor at the Ministry of Science and ICT.
