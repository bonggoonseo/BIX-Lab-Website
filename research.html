What We Study

Digital transformation and AI are changing how organizations decide, how services are delivered, and how people experience them. We study the people and organizations who use technology — not only the technology itself.

Research areas

Four Connected Themes

01

Behavior & Experience

Customer and user behavior, UX/CX, experience design, and design thinking.

Methods — surveys, interviews, experiments, user testing, customer journey analysis

Outputs — user research reports, service improvement proposals, journal papers

고객·사용자 행동, UX/CX, 경험 설계, 디자인 사고
02

Data & AI Analytics

Data analysis, AI applications, user and market insight, and decision-making.

Methods — statistics, text analytics, visualization, machine learning, evaluation of AI use

Outputs — analysis code, dashboards, data-driven proposals

데이터 분석, AI 활용, 사용자·시장 인사이트, 의사결정
03

Service & Innovation

Digital service planning, platform business, and service innovation.

Methods — case studies, service blueprints, business model analysis, prototype validation

Outputs — service plans, prototypes, innovation case studies

디지털 서비스 기획, 플랫폼 비즈니스, 서비스 혁신
04

Policy & Strategy

Public data, digital policy, industry and technology strategy, and policy evaluation.

Methods — public data analysis, policy literature review, comparative studies, evaluation design

Outputs — policy briefs, policy evaluation reports, industry–public research

공공데이터, 디지털 정책, 산업·기술 전략, 정책평가
